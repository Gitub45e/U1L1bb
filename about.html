About the "One Day" Project

Documenting Life on Our Planet

Project Overview

The "One Day" project is a collaborative effort to document a single day on Earth through photography, video, and storytelling. Participants from around the world are encouraged to capture their experiences and share them with the global community.

This unique initiative aims to showcase the incredible diversity of life, culture, and landscapes across our planet, all captured within a 24-hour period.
