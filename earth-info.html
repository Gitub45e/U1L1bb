About Our Planet

Understanding Earth and the Life It Sustains

Earth: Our Home

Earth is the third planet from the sun and the only world known to harbor life. This home of ours has vast oceans, a breathable atmosphere, & a dynamic climate. It spins on an axis & orbits the sun once a year.

Key Facts About Earth

Age

Approximately 4.5 billion years old

Distance from the Sun

About 93 million miles (150 million kilometers)

Surface Area

510 million square kilometers

Population

Over 8 billion people

Atmosphere

Composed primarily of nitrogen (78%) and oxygen (21%)

Water Coverage

Approximately 71% of Earth's surface

Incredible Biodiversity

Earth is home to millions of species of plants, animals, & microorganisms. From the depths of the ocean to the highest mountains, life thrives in remarkably diverse ecosystems.

  • Rainforests: Home to over 80% of the world's terrestrial species
  • Oceans: Cover 71% of Earth and contain 99% of the planet's living space
  • Deserts: Despite harsh conditions, host unique adapted species
  • Grasslands: Support vast herds of herbivores and their predators
  • Mountains: Contain distinct ecosystems at different altitudes

Why Earth Matters

Understanding & protecting our planet is crucial for the survival and wellbeing of all life on Earth. The interconnectedness of all living things means that actions affecting one part of the environment can have far-reaching consequences.

Key Environmental Issues:

  • Climate change and global warming
  • Deforestation and habitat loss
  • Ocean pollution and acidification
  • Air and water pollution
  • Species extinction and biodiversity loss
