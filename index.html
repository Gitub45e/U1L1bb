About the Project

The "One Day" project aims to create a comprehensive snapshot of life on Earth by collecting contributions from people of all ages, backgrounds, and locations. By documenting a single day, we hope to highlight the diversity and interconnectedness of our planet's inhabitants.

Earth Day

Earth Day is an annual event celebrated on April 22nd to demonstrate support for environmental protection. It was first celebrated in 1970 and has since grown into a very global movement, with millions of people participating worldwide.

Why This Project Matters

Every place on Earth has a story worth sharing. By bringing together images, voices, and experiences from different communities, we create a more complete picture of life on our planet.
