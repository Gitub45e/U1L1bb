One Day On Earth

Earth is the third planet from the sun and the only world known to harbor life. This home of ours has vast oceans, a breathable atmosphere, and a dynamic climate. It spins on an axis and orbits the sun once a year

This is a website is just for documenting a single day on Earth.

About the Project

The "One Day" project aims to create a comprehensive snapshot of life on Earth by collecting contributions from people of all ages, backgrounds, and locations. By documenting a single day, we hope to highlight the diversity and interconnectedness of our planet's inhabitants.

    Earth Day

    Earth Day is an annual event celebrated on April 22nd to demonstrate support for environmental protection. It was first celebrated in 1970 and has since grown into a global movement, with millions of people participating in activities to raise awareness about environmental issues.
